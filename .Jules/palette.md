## 2026-03-11 - Hover state visibility on icon buttons
**Learning:** Icon buttons in mapped lists (like the Dashboard question cards) may have incomplete or mismatched hover states (e.g., missing background highlights and border transitions) compared to their siblings, leading to a degraded interactive experience.
**Action:** When adding or reviewing icon-only buttons, ensure consistent application of hover states (`hover:bg-[color]`, `hover:border-[color]`) and focus rings (`focus-visible:ring-2`) to match the surrounding design system.
