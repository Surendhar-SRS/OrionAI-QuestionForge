## 2024-05-14 - Missing ARIA Labels on Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Found a widespread pattern across the application (`Sidebar.tsx`, `CourseSetup.tsx`, `Dashboard.tsx`) where icon-only buttons (like theme toggles, close buttons, view mode selectors, and action icons) lack `aria-label` and `title` attributes. They also frequently lack `focus-visible` styling, making keyboard navigation difficult or invisible. Another critical learning was that hover-revealed action buttons (like those on generated questions) remain invisible to keyboard users unless `focus-within:opacity-100` is applied to their container.
**Action:** Always check newly created or existing icon-only buttons for `aria-label`, `title`, and `focus-visible` styling in this project. When buttons are revealed on hover, ensure they are also revealed on `focus-within`.
